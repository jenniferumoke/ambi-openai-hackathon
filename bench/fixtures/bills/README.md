# Maple Neighbourhood Collective - accounts inbox

Snapshot: **1 November 2026, 09:00 GMT**. Prepared by Rowan Vale, volunteer coordinator.

This folder is the collective's small accounts backlog ahead of the November repair café. The invoices arrived as PDF attachments and were filed by payment status. Some of the emails arrived after the original invoice, so read them together when deciding what still needs attention.

## Folder guide

- `pending/`: supplier invoices not yet marked as settled.
- `paid/`: a previously settled invoice and its receipt. Keep for reference; a receipt is not a new bill.
- `correspondence/`: email exports associated with individual invoices.
- `outputs/`: save your proposed payment-plan artifact here, as specified in TASK.md.

## Handling notes

Use the **invoice reference**, not the email subject or a supplier's account number, to identify a bill. The amount due and due date are on each invoice. Dates in this snapshot are fixed; do not reinterpret them using today's date. The status on the receipt takes precedence over the original amount charged on the paid invoice. A reminder refers to the original invoice and does not create another obligation.

The collective keeps its paperwork even after payment. Do not move or edit the originals. There is no connected bank account, payment portal or email account in this workspace. Prepare a plan only.

Every organization, person, address, reference and transaction in this folder is fictional AMBI-Bench data. Email domains use the reserved `.example` suffix. Payment coordinates are deliberately omitted. These documents must not be submitted to a real payment or accounting service.
