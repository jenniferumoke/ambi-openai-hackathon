Meet Ambi. A voice-first companion that turns a request into visible action.

On the Mac, Ambi opens Calculator and completes a calculation. This separate recorded test shows nine times nine, with eighty-one displayed in the app.

On Windows, Ambi works in Notepad. This recorded test includes an editing mistake, recovery, and a saved note whose text was independently checked.

A coloured frame and a separate Stop task button show when Ambi is acting. Stop was tested on a separate task, and the frame cleared.

Connections offer browser handoffs to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Authenticated chat automation is not demonstrated here.

Built and tested on Mac and Windows. Linux is a source preview. Ambi is a working prototype, with clear controls and room to grow.
