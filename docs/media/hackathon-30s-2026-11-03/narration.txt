Meet Ambi, an open-source AI agent built at the London Codex Community Hackathon on the first of November. Powered by OpenAI, she brings voice and computer use together on Mac and Windows. Useful context stays in local memory, so you can pick up where you left off. Ask for help, watch her work, and stop a task with a click. This is our working prototype. Explore the code and help build what comes next.
